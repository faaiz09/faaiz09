I'm Faaiz, though most people call me Fizzy. I build the parts of software people actually touch: at [Frog8](https://frog8.co.in) that means the screens on self-service kiosks in transit and banking deployments, where someone with a train to catch has to get it right on the first try. Outside of that I make things move with Three.js, WebGL and Blender.
